- **Engi 1:** Hydro, PGs, Airfac, PGs
- **Engi 2:** Hill+water
- **Engi 3:** Side to hydro
- **Engi 4:** Water
- **Engi 5:** Ramp
- **Engi 6+:** Navy, assist, especially PGs
