## Ultimate Seton’s Guide

## An in depth guide to everything Seton's

The intended audience for this guide is everyone from complete newbies to advanced level players.To facilitate that, the document is split into sections for different rating ranges.
Feel free to use the document outline to skip to the parts you are interested in, but be aware that later sections build on the concepts discussed in the earlier ones.
Knowledge of the game’s terminology and slang are assumed. Check out the Glossary in the FAF Wiki if you are ever confused.
This document is still work in progress but everything up until the big WIP warning is finished.
Current progress: ● DONE - Beginner Guide (<1k) ● DONE - Novice Guide (~1k) ● WIP - Intermediate Guide (~1k-1.5k) ● EMPTY - Advanced Guide (1.5k-2k) ● EMPTY - Expert Guide (>2k)

## Assumptions

Playing to Win/Playing Optimally. For the purpose of comparison, whatever makes you more likely to win will be rated as the better choice.
Default Map and Settings. 4v4, Full Share, 1500 unit cap, share unit cap to allies on death, the old school non-faf, non-symmetric “scmp_009” Seton’s Clutch map. These are the canonical settings for Seton’s. All high rated games and tournaments are played using them.
