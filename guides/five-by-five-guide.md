# The 5x5 guide

[The original](https://app.notion.com/p/5x5-Guide-2ae95eec16af8024a90cf3144688d236).

## General approach

- On land-based maps, you would usually go for land factories only, no air factory.
- 5x5 maps usually are decided at the T1 stage, they’re meant to end within the first 10 minutes.
- Upgrading Mexes, Factories, and even your ACU should be punished, as this should result in your opponent having more tanks.
    - That being said, this requires constantly spending all your mass. If you flow your mass while your opponent spends their mass, this might not apply. So keep your mass bar as empty as possible without stalling.

The general approach of not “ecoing” and “teching” applies to most of the 5x5 maps. However, there are some that have to be played differently. A common example is “Desert Arena”, which features roughly 50 Mexes, and also tens of thousands of reclaim. On these kind of maps featuring large amounts of reclaim or mexes, you might want to tech up.

## Initial build order

### Hydro start

- Land Factory
- Core Mexes (up to 4)
- Hydro (2x engineer + ACU)

### Regular start

- Land Factory
- 2x Pgen
- 2x Mex
- 2x Pgen
- 2x Mex

### Your first factory

- 5x5 maps are super small. You won’t get away with only building engineers on your initial factory. You want to *-be aggressive, and at the same time you need to cover your expanding engineers.
- A solid way to start is something like: 3x engineer, 1x tank, 1x scout, 1x engineer, 1x tank, 1x scout, 1x engineer, 1x tank, 1x scout, infinite engineers - by then, at least your second factory should be online to provide you with constant reinforcements.

### More Factories

- Rule of thumb:
    - Keep all production in your main base, 5x5 is too small to go for any expansion
        - Exception: A single reclaim factory for attack moving mass fields
    - Queue more factories if you have mass in storage, stop building them if you are (about to run) out of mass.
    - Use the build range of your engineers as much as possible, causing travel time equals delaying your tanks.
    - You ain’t supposed to upgrade Mexes, so make use of adjacency (e.g. placing your land factories next to a Mex) as much as possible.
    - Use way points. And spread them across the map covering all lanes.
        - You need to constantly adjust them throughout the game:
            - If you gain map control, move them forward
            - If you lose map control, move them backward
- How to queue more factories without stalling energy:
    - 1x Pgen > 1x Land Factory (if you build the Pgen before the land factory, you will never run out of power for starting the production right away.

## Reclaim

- Use it to boost your start (e.g. if there are tree groups all over the map, you might be able to initially skip some Pgens in order to get more factories done quicker)
- Use it to boost your amount of factories by reclaiming wrecks from fighting
    - If you run out of reclaim, pause some of them.
    - If you get access to reclaim again, unpause them.

## ACU

- Your ACU has the build power of 2x T1 engineer, BUT: It also equals 20x T1 tank. Therefore, you want to get it to the front line fairly early on a 5x5 map.
- Usually you want to keep your ACU between your base and the enemy ACU to prevent getting cut off from your reinforcements.
- Try to keep your ACU in front of your army and try to farm enemy units. Thereby, your ACU will gain veterancy, which means having more HP and better regeneration.
- Use overcharge if possible
    - Requires 1x energy storage and some sort of energy filling up the storage
        - either “overbuild” some T1 Pgens
        - or reclaim trees
    - 7.5K energy are required for one overcharge
    - Overcharge has an area of effect, aim for groups of units

## Intel

- Intel is king:
    - Use land scouts.
    - Use radars.
        - T1 radar: 20 energy / tick
        - T2 radar: 150 energy / tick (even if placed defensively, it covers almost the whole 5x5 map)

## Engagement

- If you have more tanks in one place, go fight.
    - You want to strategically rally units more towards one place to create the advantage if necessary.
- If you have fewer tanks in one place, retreat, and reinforce.
- Raid mexes, and only go for the main base if you are confident you could crush it

## Unit Mix

- Tanks. Tanks. And Tanks. Accompanied by the odd land scout to provide intel.
    - A “good” mix would be something like: 1x Tank, 1x Scout, 5x Tank.
- Special purpose:
    - If your opponent goes for static point defenses, include 1x mobile artillery.
    - If your opponent goes for an air factory, include 1x mobile anti-air.

## Static Defenses

- Avoid them, they are expensive. You want to have tanks.
- Build them if necessary, e.g. if
    - you have fewer units and/or need to defend an expansion or your base
    - you end up having your ACU at the other side of the map while they push you with their ACU (T1 Point Defense outranges an unupgraded ACU)
    - both you and your opponent contest a reclaim field
- Exception from the rule: Walls. They’re cheap, and can be used to block pathways. Your opponent has to target/reclaim them one-by-one. If you want to engage yourself, you could easily select and “CTRL + K” them.

## Win Conditions

- Aggression
- Better engagements
- Gun ACU*
- T2 Land*
- Air*

* You usually opt for one of these if you’re facing kind of a stalemate situation.
