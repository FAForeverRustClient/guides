# Zeuthea Valley

A 1v1 build order by Sladow-Noob.

You spawn at the bottom.

## Build

- **ACU:** Hydrorush
- **or:** fac - PG - 3 mex. The first engineer builds the last mex.
- **Engi 2:** Reclaim small rocks, get back mexes
- **Engi 3:** Reclaim above fac
- **Engi 4:** Assist
- **Engi 5+:** Expand

## Notes

- Mix in more tanks to play aggressive.
- Use transports.
- The ACU goes to the right side.
